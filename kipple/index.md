# kipple

<p class="lead">A plain-text day planner for iPhone. It piles up when you're not looking. Clear it.</p>

<div class="terminal" role="img" aria-label="Example of kipple's plain-text file">
<span class="d"># 2026-11-12</span><br>
<span class="k">[x]</span> Write the status update<br>
<span class="k">[ ]</span> Review open pull requests @14:30<br>
<span class="k">[ ]</span> Water the plants (every 3 days)<br>
<span class="k">[&gt;]</span> Book the dentist
</div>

kipple keeps your days in one plain-text file, `plan.txt`, the way John Carmack kept his `.plan`: tasks grouped by day, ticked off with `[x]`.

Tasks left open on past days are **kipple**. They pile up when you're not looking, and the counter tells you exactly how much. Finish them, move them, or let them go.

## What it does

- One plain-text file you own, synced through your iCloud Drive
- Today first, a week strip to jump between days, drag a task onto a day to move it
- Reminders (`@14:30`) and repeating tasks (`(every mon)`)
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts
- No account, no tracking, no ads
