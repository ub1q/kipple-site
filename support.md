## Frequently asked questions

**What is kipple?**
Open tasks left on past days, plus tasks you've postponed twice or more in two weeks. The counter shows how much has piled up. Finish, move or cancel them to clear it.

**Where are my tasks stored?**
In one plain-text file, `plan.txt`. With iCloud Drive on, it's in kipple's folder in your iCloud Drive and syncs between your devices. You can open and edit it in any text editor.

**How do I add a reminder?**
Long-press a task › Remind at…, or type a time in the task: `Call the dentist @14:30`.

**How do repeating tasks work?**
Long-press a task › Repeat, or type a rule at the end: `(every day)`, `(every weekday)`, `(every 3 days)`, `(every mon, thu)`, `(every month)`. When you finish one, the next one appears on its next date. A missed repeat is marked skipped, so it doesn't pile up as kipple.

**My widget doesn't update.**
Open kipple once; widgets refresh when the plan changes. If you ticked tasks off in the widget, the app picks those changes up when it opens.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete the app, and delete `plan.txt` from iCloud Drive if you used sync. kipple keeps no data anywhere else.

**Does kipple work on iPad or Mac?**
Not yet. kipple 1.0 is for iPhone.
