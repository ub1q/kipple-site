# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: {{ site.policy_updated }}_

kipple is a plain-text task planner for iPhone, made by {{ site.owner.name }}.

## The short version

kipple doesn't collect, store or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## Your tasks

- Your tasks are saved in a plain-text file (`plan.txt`) on your iPhone.
- If you use iCloud Drive, the file is also saved to **your own** iCloud Drive and synced by Apple between your devices. Apple's privacy policy covers this; the developer can't see your iCloud Drive.
- If you choose your own sync file, kipple reads and writes only that file.
- The kipple widget and the "Add to kipple" share extension read and write the same file, on your device.

## Notifications

Reminders and the morning summary are **local notifications**. They're scheduled on your iPhone from your own tasks and are never sent to a server.

## Siri and Shortcuts

When you use kipple with Siri or Shortcuts, the request is handled by Apple's systems under Apple's privacy policy. kipple only reads or adds tasks in your file on the device.

## Data we collect

None. kipple contains no third-party SDKs, analytics or crash-reporting services. If you've agreed to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports. These contain no task contents.

## This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and secure the service; see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

## Your rights

Because kipple doesn't collect personal data, there's nothing held about you to access, correct or delete. Your tasks stay in your own file; deleting the app or the file removes them. If you have questions about your rights under the GDPR, contact us below.

## Children

kipple doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
